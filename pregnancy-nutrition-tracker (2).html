📖 How to Log Foods for Best Tracking

💡 Pro Tip

The app recognizes foods by keywords. The more specific you are, the better it tracks nutrients!

✅ Good Examples:

Instead of "breakfast" → Write "2 idlis with sambar, coconut chutney"
Instead of "curry" → Write "spinach curry" or "mixed vegetable curry with carrots, beans, peas"
Instead of "tiffin" → Write "dosa with potato filling and coconut chutney"
Instead of "appam" → Write "appam made from rice batter with coconut milk"

🔍 Foods the App Recognizes (Including Tamil Names!)

Protein Sources:

Dal, paruppu, lentils, beans, chickpeas, rajma, chana, moong, urad, toor, egg (muttai), milk (paal), curd (thayir), paneer, tofu, nuts, sambar, rasam, kootu, sundal, usili

Iron Sources:

Spinach, keerai (all types: arai, mulai, ponnanganni, thandu), methi, beetroot, ragi (kezhvaragu), jaggery (vellam), dates, curry leaves (karivepillai), drumstick (murungakkai), pomegranate, egg

Calcium Sources:

Milk (paal), curd (thayir), paneer, cheese, ragi (kezhvaragu), sesame (ellu), almonds (badam), coconut (thengai), soy

Fiber Sources:

Vegetables (kari), poriyal, kootu, kuzhambu, avial, olan, fruits, whole wheat, brown rice, oats, dal (paruppu), idli, dosa, appam, ragi, bajra (kambu)

Carbohydrates:

Rice (sadam, arisi), roti, chapati, idli, dosa, appam, puttu, upma (uppuma), pongal, idiyappam, sevai, adai, paniyaram, potato (urulaikizhangu), banana (vazhaipazham)

B12 & Omega-3:

Eggs (muttai), fortified milk (paal), curd (thayir, mor), walnuts, flaxseeds, chia seeds

📝 Sample Log Entries with Tamil Foods

Breakfast Example 1 (Tamil Style):

- 3 idlis with sambar (toor dal, vegetables)
- Thengai (coconut) chutney
- 1 glass paal (milk)
- 1 vazhaipazham (banana)

Breakfast Example 2 (Pongal):

- Ven pongal (rice and moong dal)
- Coconut chutney
- 1 muttai (boiled egg)
- 1 cup thayir (curd)

Lunch Example (Complete Tamil Meal):

- Rice sadam (2 cups)
- Sambar (toor dal with drumstick, carrot, beans)
- Beans poriyal (with coconut)
- Keerai kootu (spinach with dal)
- Rasam
- Thayir (curd)
- Papad

Lunch Example 2 (Mixed):

- Chapati or roti (2)
- Vendakkai (okra) kuzhambu
- Carrot beans poriyal
- Dal or paruppu
- Mor (buttermilk)

Dinner Example:

- Dosa (2) or appam (2)
- Kurma (mixed vegetables with coconut)
- Keerai (spinach) poriyal
- 1 muttai (egg) omelet or boiled
- 1 glass paal (milk)

Snack Example:

- Sundal (chickpea or kadalai with coconut)
- 1 vazhaipazham (banana)
- Handful of badam (almonds) and akhrot (walnuts)
OR
- Ragi (kezhvaragu) koozh or porridge
- 3-4 dates with milk

🥬 Common Tamil Vegetables & Greens (Keerai)

All keerai varieties are EXCELLENT for iron and folate!

  • Arai keerai (Amaranth spinach) - Very high in iron
  • Mulai keerai (Tender amaranth) - Rich in calcium
  • Ponnanganni keerai - Great for hemoglobin
  • Thandu keerai (Stem amaranth) - High fiber
  • Palak / Pasalai keerai (Spinach) - Iron rich
  • Murungakkai keerai (Drumstick leaves) - Super nutritious
  • Methi keerai (Fenugreek) - Iron and folate

Log as: "keerai poriyal", "keerai kootu", or specify the type!

✨ Remember

  • Mention ingredients, not just dish names
  • Include quantities when possible (2 rotis, 1 bowl, 1 cup)
  • Separate items with hyphens (-) or new lines
  • Be specific about vegetables and grains used
